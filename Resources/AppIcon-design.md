# FastOCR 图标

由内置 image_gen 生成。AppIcon.png 是透明背景原稿；AppIcon.icns 包含 16–1024 像素的 macOS 图标。运行 `./scripts/build-icon.sh` 可从原稿重新生成 icns；应用打包脚本自动复制 icns。

## 最终提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished premium macOS app icon for FastOCR, a local screen-to-text utility. Square 1024x1024 PNG with true transparent background outside the icon. A large macOS rounded-square squircle tile centered occupying 86% of canvas, rich cobalt blue to slightly indigo subtle gradient, restrained dimensional beveled edge, beautiful soft studio light. Central bold white scan-viewfinder symbol made of four rounded L-shaped corner brackets. Within brackets a beautifully proportioned white capital T with two short horizontal text strokes below, instantly communicates extracting text. A single subtle electric cyan scanning beam crosses lower third of central glyph, no glow outside tile. Minimal sophisticated native desktop utility aesthetic, crisp precise geometric silhouette, optical balance, excellent readability at 32px. Front facing orthographic, no perspective. No wordmark, no app name, no other letters, no watermark, no extra floating objects, no background scene, no mockup. Keep all corners and shadow within canvas. Deliver icon alone.
